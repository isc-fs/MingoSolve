# Data licence

Parts of this repository are derived from the [FS-Quiz](https://fs-quiz.eu) question bank, which is made available
under the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

Those parts are published under the same licence:

- `data/examples.toml`: past questions (id, our own short paraphrase, the command that solves it, the official answer)
- `data/known_keys.toml`: official answers known not to match the physics or the rules
- `data/past_questions.toml`: a fingerprint of every bank question, generated by `legacy/python/scripts/export_past.py`:
  question id, the quizzes it appeared in, the official answer only when it is a short number with a unit, and sorted
  32-bit hashes of word 3-grams and of the numbers. Hashes, ids and numeric answers only: no question text can be
  read back from it. The app uses it to recognise a pasted question
- `docs/archetypes/`: the analysis of the calculation questions by archetype
- `tests/golden/`: snapshots generated from the above

Attribution: contains information from the FS-Quiz question bank (fs-quiz.eu), made available under the ODbL 1.0.

If you reuse or adapt these files and publish the result, keep this attribution and share the adapted database under
the ODbL. Question text is not reproduced here; each row points to its FS-Quiz question id.
